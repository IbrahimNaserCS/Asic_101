1. The die is the physical area of the chip in its entirety, It is the outer physical boundary of the chip.
2. The core is the region where the cells are actually placed. 
3. Rows exist to keep the cells organized and placed in a standardized way. 
4. Because utilization is how much the area of the cells takes up the total area of the core. If utilization is low that means that a very small amount of the core is made of cells so therefore there is more whitespace.
5. There are tap cells which keep transistor bodies tied to power cells. Then there are endcap cells which are used to terminate rows.
